Selected direction

Ember, refined for practice.

01 Ember sets the direction for the product: warm paper, charcoal, the orange wordmark, and clear practice actions. The implemented dashboard keeps the palette and removes decorative desk imagery. The other boards remain as reference explorations.

These are visual concepts. The AI avatar, code execution, metrics, and example content illustrate the proposed experience; the boards do not indicate that those features are implemented.

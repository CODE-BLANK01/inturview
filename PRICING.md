# Pricing architecture

The product serves two different customers and must price them separately.

## Candidates

Candidates use inturview to learn interview formats, practice answers, and improve through repeated debriefs. They do not need seats, workspaces, annual contracts, or hiring analytics.

### Free — $0

- 2 recruiter screens per month
- 3 behavioral sessions per month
- 5 coding interviews per month
- 2 system-design sessions per month
- Full debriefs and interview history

Free remains deliberately useful while the product finds retention. Tighten limits only after usage data shows repeated value and meaningful marginal cost.

### Interview Sprint — $19 for 30 days

- Unlimited recruiter, behavioral, coding, and system-design practice
- Full debriefs and interview history
- Repeat weak rounds without monthly caps
- One payment; no annual commitment or automatic yearly contract

Interview preparation is urgent and episodic. A 30-day pass matches the candidate's job better than team tiers or an annual subscription. Billing is not live yet, so this plan remains a launch list until checkout exists.

## Employers: Inturview Hire

Employers create role-specific AI screening interviews, invite candidates, compare evidence-backed scorecards, and shortlist people for human interviews. Employer-invited screening results belong to that hiring workflow. A candidate's private practice history is never part of an employer shortlist.

### Design partner pilot

- First role
- First 10 completed employer-invited screens free
- Founder-led interview and rubric setup
- Scorecard and shortlist review with the hiring team

### Validation price

- $10 per completed employer-invited screen
- No recruiter-seat fee
- Charge only for completed interviews
- Custom volume pricing after 100 completed screens per month

Do not add multiple employer tiers before the company workflow has design partners and repeat usage. The first pricing question is whether employers will repeatedly pay per completed screen for a useful shortlist.

## Measurement

Candidate conversion is `signup → interview_started → debrief_completed → another debrief`. Test payment only after candidates complete a debrief.

Employer conversion is `role_created → candidate_invited → screening_completed → candidate_shortlisted`. Track time saved, shortlist acceptance, completion rate, and how many shortlisted candidates reach a human interview.

## Market context

As of September 2026, [Aced lists AI interview membership](https://www.tryexponent.com/upgrade) at $99 monthly or $20 per month on annual billing, while [TestGorilla starts employer plans](https://www.testgorilla.com/pricing/) at $142 per month annually and reserves fully customized AI interviews for plans starting at $400 per month. Inturview's initial prices stay deliberately simpler because it has not yet proven comparable breadth or employer retention.
